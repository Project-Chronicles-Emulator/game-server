Exploration destinations

ArcheAge Chronicles researched demo build · Static client catalog and authored map exports

Start in the main world with /gm tp_map 9012, then use a destination's /gm tp command. Coordinates are meters. Commands assume your current map belongs to ArcheWorld; they do not load a dungeon or another world.

To reveal saved map fog, use /gm reveal_map and select your character again. Fog reveal leaves Adventure Progress, quests, zone restrictions and collision unchanged. Approximate destinations use a real authored height but may be an NPC location or outside the named area's true polygon. Landing safety and traversal are unverified.

Every named Zone catalog entry is retained, including disabled and unplaced areas. Checkmarks are optional notes saved only in this browser; they do not modify game progress.
